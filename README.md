📊 Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard
Recently Learned: Interactive Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard. Developed an interactive Power BI Dashboard to analyze Super Store Sales performance.

Used DAX, data modeling, KPIs, filters, and interactive visualizations to identify business trends and insights.

I’m excited to share that I recently learned how to design and analyze an interactive Super Store Sales Dashboard using data visualization and business intelligence techniques.

Through this dashboard, I learned how to analyze sales performance using important KPIs and charts, including:

📌 KPI's:
🔹 Total Sales – $2M

🔹 Total Profit – $286.40K

🔹 Total Orders – 509

🔹 Sales by Region – West, East, Central & South

🔹 Sales by State – Geographical sales analysis

🔹 Sales by Category & Sub-Category

🔹 Sales by Segment – Consumer, Corporate & Home Office

🔹 Monthly & Yearly Sales Trends

🔹 Sales by Ship Mode

🔑 Key Insights:
🔹 Total Sales: Generated approximately $2M in total sales.

🔹 Total Profit: Achieved around $286.40K in profit.

🔹 Total Orders: Recorded 509 total orders.

🔹 Top Region: West contributed the highest sales share.

🔹 Top Category: Technology was the best-performing category.

🔹 Top Sub-Categories: Phones and Chairs were among the top sellers.

🔹 Top Segment: Consumer generated the highest sales.

🔹 Sales Trend: Sales showed an overall upward trend year-over-year.
